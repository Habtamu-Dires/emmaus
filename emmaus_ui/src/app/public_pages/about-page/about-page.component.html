Our Story

Emmaus Companions is a board-led humanitarian organization based in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia. Guided by principles of dignity, justice, and compassion, we work to prevent violence, respond to crises, and build resilient communities.

We stand with elders, women, children, and persons with specific needs—raising awareness, offering protection and support, and advocating for lasting systemic change.

From prevention and emergency response to empowerment and policy advocacy, our programs are rooted in the communities we serve and shaped by the people who lead them.
